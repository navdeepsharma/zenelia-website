Frequently Asked Questions

What is Zenelia?

Zenelia is a daily brain training app with a personal AI coach. Each 90-second session covers four cognitive domains — working memory, processing speed, attention, and language. Your AI coach adapts every session based on your performance, mood, and sleep patterns.

Is Zenelia free?

Zenelia offers a free tier with 4 brain games and 2 sessions per week. Premium unlocks all 12 games, unlimited daily training, the full AI coach experience, monthly health reports, and all difficulty levels. You can start a 7-day free trial from the Premium tab in the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your iPhone's Settings app. Go to Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Zenelia → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → go to your Profile → tap Privacy & Data → Delete My Account. Your account will be marked for deletion immediately and your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. If you change your mind within that window, email us at hello@zenelia.ai.

How do I export my data?

Go to your Profile → Privacy & Data → Export My Data. This generates a file containing your complete training history, scores, and profile information.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your cognitive data is used only to personalize your AI coach experience and generate your health reports. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What is the ACTIVE Trial?

The ACTIVE (Advanced Cognitive Training for Independent and Vital Elderly) Trial is one of the largest randomized controlled studies of cognitive training in older adults. It followed over 2,800 participants for more than a decade and found that regular, short cognitive training sessions produced measurable improvements in mental sharpness lasting up to 10 years. Zenelia's exercises are based on the same cognitive training paradigms used in this study.

Is Zenelia a medical device?

No. Zenelia is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease. It is a cognitive training tool designed to help build a daily brain exercise habit. Consult your healthcare provider with any questions about your cognitive health.
