Inspired by 20 years of research.

Zenelia's exercises are inspired by cognitive training research, including the ACTIVE Trial, which followed more than 2,800 older adults for up to 20 years. In the 20-year follow-up, speed training plus booster sessions was linked to a 25% lower risk of a dementia diagnosis. Zenelia is inspired by this research but wasn't part of it.

Brain changes linked to Alzheimer's can begin 15 to 20 years before symptoms appear. Healthy brain habits matter at every age.
