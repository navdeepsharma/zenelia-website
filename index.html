Grounded in real science.

Zenelia's exercises are based on the same cognitive training paradigms used in the ACTIVE Trial — one of the largest and longest studies of cognitive training in older adults. That trial followed over 2,800 participants for more than a decade and found that regular, short cognitive training sessions produced measurable improvements in mental sharpness lasting up to 10 years.

The trial has been widely cited in Alzheimer's and dementia research as evidence that targeted brain exercises may support long-term cognitive health. Zenelia brings that same research-backed approach to your phone.
