Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 23, 2026

Zenelia ("we," "our," or "the app") is operated by Avasy Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Zenelia iOS application.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password. Your password is handled securely by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never stored in plain text.

When you use the app, we collect your cognitive training scores, session history, mood and sleep check-in responses, and app usage data. This data is used to personalize your AI coach experience and generate your cognitive health reports.

If you upload a profile photo, it is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and personalize the Zenelia experience, including generating AI coach messages tailored to your training history, mood, and sleep patterns. We use your email address to send transactional emails such as account confirmation, password reset, premium welcome emails, and monthly health reports (when you request them).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Zenelia uses the following third-party services to operate:

Supabase — authentication and data storage. Your account data and training progress are stored securely on Supabase's infrastructure. RevenueCat — subscription management. RevenueCat processes your subscription status but does not have access to your training data. Anthropic — AI coach messages. Your cognitive data (scores, mood, sleep) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate personalized coach messages. No personally identifiable information (name, email) is included in these requests. Resend — email delivery. Used to send transactional emails from hello@zenelia.ai.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the Privacy & Data screen in the app, your account is marked for deletion immediately and your data is permanently purged from our servers within 30 days. If you change your mind within that 30-day window, contact hello@zenelia.ai to recover your account.

Data Export

You can export all of your data at any time from the Privacy & Data screen in the app. The export includes your profile information, complete session history, domain scores, and all training data in a readable format.

Children's Privacy

Zenelia is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS), secure authentication, and access controls. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at hello@zenelia.ai.
